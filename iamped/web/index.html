Lower bitrates fit more songs on small devices. iPods use AAC; USB players use MP3. Drag the slider all the way to the right for Apple Lossless (ALAC) — exact quality, much larger files. iAmped only transcodes files above the selected preset or lossless files when transcoding is enabled.
